Team · Principal Investigator

Karen Cheung

kcheung@ece.ubc.caGoogle ScholarLinkedIn

Karen C. Cheung is a Professor at the University of British Columbia, with appointments in the Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering and the School of Biomedical Engineering. Her research focuses on developing micro- and nanoscale technologies for applications in biology and medicine, including silicon photonic biosensors, precision bioprinting, microfluidic tissue models, and biomaterials for neural repair.

She received her B.S. and Ph.D. in Bioengineering from the University of California, Berkeley, and conducted postdoctoral research at the École Polytechnique Fédérale de Lausanne (EPFL) in Switzerland before joining UBC in 2006.

Beyond her research, she has contributed to graduate education, academic leadership, and the broader scientific community. She served as Graduate Program Director of UBC’s School of Biomedical Engineering from 2017 to 2025. She is a member of the Board of Directors of the Chemical and Biological Microsystems Society (CBMS) and has been actively involved in the International Conference on Miniaturized Systems for Chemistry and Life Sciences (MicroTAS), including conference organization and scientific community initiatives. Her professional service reflects a longstanding commitment to supporting interdisciplinary research, mentorship, and the development of the micro- and nanosystems research community.
