Tanya Bennet

tanyabennet@ece.ubc.caThesis

Tanya Bennet is a PhD student developing a lung-on-chip platform with applications for identifying biomarkers for chronic obstructive pulmonary disease (COPD) and therpeutic targets. She completed her BASc in Integrated Engineering at UBC in 2016, with focus on Biomedical Engineering, Bioprocessing and Biomaterials.

Bio-Medical Micro Devices Laboratory

The Bio-Medical Micro Devices Laboratory at the University of British Columbia develops micro- and nanoscale technologies for biology and medicine, with research in biosensing, bioprinting, microfluidic tissue models, neural repair, and engineering education.
