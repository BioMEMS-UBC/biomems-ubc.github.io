Avineet Randhawa

avineet.randhawa@ubc.caThesis

Vinny completed his Bachelors in Chemical Engineering at the University of Toronto. His Masters work in Biomedical Engineering is studying how a microfluidic airway-on-a-chip platform can be used to investigate effects of aerosols on lung epithelial cells.

Bio-Medical Micro Devices Laboratory

The Bio-Medical Micro Devices Laboratory at the University of British Columbia develops micro- and nanoscale technologies for biology and medicine, with research in biosensing, bioprinting, microfluidic tissue models, neural repair, and engineering education.
