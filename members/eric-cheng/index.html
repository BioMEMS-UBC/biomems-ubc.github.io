Team · PhD Student

Eric Cheng

ericc@ece.ubc.ca

Eric Cheng is a PhD student, developing a high throughput single cell dispensing system using inkjet technology. In 2015, he completed his MASc thesis in the area of inkjet printing of cell solutions. He completed his BASc in Electrical Engineering, Biomedical Option, in 2012 at UBC.

Bio-Medical Micro Devices Laboratory

The Bio-Medical Micro Devices Laboratory at the University of British Columbia develops micro- and nanoscale technologies for biology and medicine, with research in biosensing, bioprinting, microfluidic tissue models, neural repair, and engineering education.
