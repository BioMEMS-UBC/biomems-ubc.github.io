Yansha Deng, Adam Noel, Maged Elkashlan, Arumugam Nallanathan, Karen C Cheung

arXiv preprint arXiv:1601.00681

In this paper, we present an analytical model for the diffusive molecular
communication (MC) system with a reversible adsorption receiver in a fluid environment.
The widely used concentration shift keying (CSK) is considered for modulation. The time-
varying spatial distribution of the information molecules under the reversible adsorption and
desorption reaction at the surface of a receiver is analytically characterized. Based on the
spatial distribution, we derive the net number of newly-adsorbed information molecules **…**
