Adam Noel completed his PhD in 2015 under the co-supervision of Prof. Robert Schober, working in molecular communication. He was an Assistant Professor at the University of Warwick in the United Kingdom, and is now in the Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering at Memorial University in St. John’s, Canada.
