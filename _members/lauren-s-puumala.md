Lauren is a PhD Candidate in Biomedical Engineering. Her research is focused on the development of silicon photonic biosensors for point-of-care diagnostics. Her work involves designing surface functionalization strategies, assay workflows, and fluidic delivery approaches for silicon photonic biosensors toward robust diagnostics for women's health and neurotrauma. She enjoys the interdisciplinary nature of this work, which lies at the intersection of integrated photonics, microfluidics, interfacial chemistry, and biochemistry. She completed her BASc in Integrated Engineering at UBC in 2021.

When not in the lab, Lauren can often be found enjoying the Canadian outdoors, running, cross-country skiing, hiking, or even just strolling to one of UBC's beautiful beaches, and is an avid baker and knitter.
