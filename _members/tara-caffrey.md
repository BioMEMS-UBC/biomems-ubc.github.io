Tara is the Research Facilitator for the Mend the Gap project, where she enjoys bringing together researchers and collaborators from different disciplines to support the project's shared goals. With a background in academic biomedical research, she has worked in neuroscience, stem cell models, and neurodegenerative diseases. Her current research interests include using in vitro models to explore biomaterials for spinal cord injury (SCI) repair and regeneration in Dr. Cheung’s lab.

Outside of work, Tara enjoys volunteering with youth organizations in her community, spending time tending to her vegetable and flower gardens, and exploring the beautiful landscapes of Canada's West Coast.
