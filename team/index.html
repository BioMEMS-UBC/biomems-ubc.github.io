Team

Our team brings together students and researchers with diverse expertise and perspectives to address challenges at the intersection of engineering, biology, and medicine. We value collaboration and mentorship, and are proud of our alumni who continue to make contributions in academia, industry, and medicine.

Principal Investigator

Karen Cheung

Karen C. Cheung is a Professor in the Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering and the School of Biomedical Engineering at the University of British Columbia. Her research focuses on developing micro- and nanoscale technologies for biology and medicine, spanning biosensing, microfluidics, bioprinting, and biomaterials. She received her B.S. and Ph.D. in Bioengineering from the University of California, Berkeley, and conducted postdoctoral research at EPFL in Switzerland before joining UBC in 2006.

Bio-Medical Micro Devices Laboratory

The Bio-Medical Micro Devices Laboratory at the University of British Columbia develops micro- and nanoscale technologies for biology and medicine, with research in biosensing, bioprinting, microfluidic tissue models, neural repair, and engineering education.
