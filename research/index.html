Research projects

Research

We harness the unique capabilities of micro- and nanoscale engineering to develop technologies for biology and medicine, bridging advances in engineering with new opportunities for scientific discovery and biomedical innovation.

01

Silicon Photonic Biosensors for Accessible Molecular Measurements

We develop miniature optical sensors to measure multiple biological molecules on a single chip. By combining silicon photonics, molecular recognition assays, and microfluidics, we aim to make molecular measurements more accessible and support the study of changing biological processes.

Explore this project

02

Precision Bioprinting and Single-Cell Technologies

We combine inkjet printing, real-time optical imaging, and machine learning to dispense individual living cells with greater accuracy and efficiency. Our work connects fundamental studies of cell motion and fluid mechanics with automated workflows for single-cell genomic and proteomic analysis.

Explore this project

03

Microfluidic Tissue Models and Organ-on-Chip Systems

We build microfluidic platforms that recreate selected features of cellular environments under controlled laboratory conditions. Integrating three-dimensional cultures, engineered extracellular matrices, and quantitative imaging, we investigate how oxygen availability, fluid flow, and tissue interfaces influence cell behaviour and responses to treatment.

Explore this project

04

Engineering Biomaterials for Neural Repair

We investigate how engineered hydrogels and their physical properties influence neural growth and regeneration. As part of the interdisciplinary Mend the Gap initiative, we combine microscale neural tissue models, hydrogel characterization, and quantitative imaging to inform biomaterial-based approaches to spinal cord injury repair.

Explore this project

05

Engineering Education and Inclusion

Through collaborative studies of STEM outreach, engineering identity, and educational program design, we examine how learning experiences can broaden participation in engineering. We also investigate educational approaches that prepare engineers for interdisciplinary and translational biomedical practice.

Explore this project

Bio-Medical Micro Devices Laboratory

The Bio-Medical Micro Devices Laboratory at the University of British Columbia develops micro- and nanoscale technologies for biology and medicine, with research in biosensing, bioprinting, microfluidic tissue models, neural repair, and engineering education.
